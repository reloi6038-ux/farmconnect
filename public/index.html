Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about buying and selling on FarmConnect

Register as a farmer, complete your profile, and use the "Add Produce" button on your dashboard. Fill in product name, category, quantity, price, and location — your listing goes live instantly and is visible to buyers across Nigeria.
Buyers browse the marketplace, filter by category, state, price range, and quality grade. Clicking any listing shows full details plus a "Send Enquiry" button. Your enquiry reaches the farmer directly — no middlemen, no delays.
Market prices are sourced from regular surveys of major regional markets (Mile 12 Lagos, Dawanau Kano, Onitsha, etc.). They are informational benchmarks, not guaranteed transactable prices. Always verify with the specific farmer before a large purchase.
FarmConnect is currently a connection platform. Buyers and farmers negotiate directly via enquiries, then agree on payment method between themselves — typically bank transfer or cash on delivery. We plan to add escrow payment integration soon.
Verified farmers have submitted identity or farm documentation that our team has reviewed. Their listings show a green checkmark. This signals trust and reliability but is not a formal guarantee — always do your own diligence for large transactions.
Delivery is coordinated directly between the farmer and buyer after an enquiry is accepted. Many farmers offer delivery to major cities. For bulk orders, third-party logistics partners can be arranged. Delivery costs and timing are agreed during negotiation.
Contact our support team using the contact form. Include screenshots of the enquiry conversation and any relevant documentation. We review disputes and can suspend accounts that repeatedly violate platform policies. However, FarmConnect does not handle refunds or payments directly.
Three roles: Farmers (sellers — can list produce, respond to enquiries, track performance), Buyers (purchasers — can browse, favourite, send enquiries, leave reviews), and Admins (platform staff — manage users, listings, market data). Farmer and buyer accounts are self-registered and free forever.
