Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026

What We Collect

Account info (name, email, phone, location), listings, enquiries, favorites, and messages sent through the contact form.

How We Use It

To operate the platform, connect farmers with buyers, generate notifications, and moderate content.

Protection

Passwords are hashed with bcrypt and never exposed. All traffic uses HTTPS in production. Sessions use signed JWTs.

Sharing

Contact details are visible only to parties in an active enquiry. We never sell personal data.

Your Rights

You can update or delete your data via your profile page. To delete your account, contact support.

Cookies

We use localStorage to store your session token. No third-party tracking cookies.
