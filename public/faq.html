Frequently Asked Questions

How do farmers list produce?

Register as a farmer, then use your dashboard's "Add Produce" button. Fill in product name, category, quantity, price, and location. Your listing goes live instantly.

How do buyers find produce?

Browse the marketplace. Use filters for category, state, price range, and organic status. Sort by newest, price, or popularity. Click any card to see full details and contact the farmer.

How do enquiries work?

Buyers send an enquiry with quantity and offer price. The farmer receives a notification, responds, and can accept or reject. Both parties see status updates in real time.

Are market prices accurate?

Market prices are informational, sourced from regular surveys. Always verify with farmers before large purchases — they reflect regional averages, not guaranteed transactable prices.

What account types exist?

Farmer (sells produce), Buyer (purchases produce), and Admin (platform moderation). Farmer and buyer accounts are self-registered; admins are created only by platform staff.

What does verification mean?

Verified farmers have submitted identity or farm documentation, reviewed by our team. Their listings display a blue check. It signals trust but is not a guarantee — always do your own diligence on large orders.

How is my data protected?

Passwords are hashed with bcrypt. Sessions use JWT. Personal contact details are only visible to verified enquiring parties. See our Privacy Policy for details.
