About FarmConnect

Nigeria's premium farm-to-market platform

Our Mission

FarmConnect connects smallholder farmers directly to buyers — restaurants, retailers, processors, and bulk purchasers. We eliminate inefficient middlemen, ensure fair prices, and build a transparent agricultural supply chain across Africa.

Our Values

  • Transparency — real market prices and farmer asking prices side by side
  • Directness — buyers talk to farmers, no intermediaries
  • Trust — verified farmer accounts, verified listings
  • Accessibility — designed for every farmer, from smallholder to commercial

Why It Matters

Nigeria's agricultural sector employs millions but suffers from fragmented markets, information asymmetry, and price volatility. FarmConnect brings efficiency to this sector by creating a single marketplace where farmers and buyers transact fairly and directly.
